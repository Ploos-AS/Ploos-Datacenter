# Roadmap

| Phase | Deliverable | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Feasibility | Written site, power, fiber and building quotes; approved business case |
| 1 | First rack | Electrical and cooling acceptance, monitoring, restore and outage tests |
| 2 | Internal workloads and private VPS beta | Tenant isolation, abuse handling, billing and provisioning tested |
| 3 | Public VPS launch | Capacity limits, support coverage, terms, privacy and recovery verified |
| 4 | Second rack | Demand and cash flow justify expansion |
| 5 | Further modules and network autonomy | Separate investment approval |

Do not promise high availability from a single site. Keep off-site backups and independent DNS/monitoring. Public VPS launch requires operational readiness, not merely installed hardware.
