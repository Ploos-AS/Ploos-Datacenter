# Site and facility requirements

Preliminary targets, not engineering specifications.

- Prefer 500–1000 m² with commercial zoning, road access, drainage, expansion space and affordable power and fiber.
- Compare purchase, lease, existing industrial space, used telecom shelters and prefabricated technical buildings.
- Initial module: approximately 20–30 m², one or two 42U racks, adequate service clearances and certified floor loading.
- Initial IT load 2–5 kW; request quotes for expansion to 10–20 kW. Distinguish IT load from total facility demand.
- Qualified contractors must design electrical protection, grounding, UPS, cooling, fire detection and any suppression. Evaluate generator connection and safe battery installation.
- Assess snow, flood, frost, condensation, noise, physical access, insurance and planning permission.
- Request business fiber with symmetric bandwidth, routed IPv6 and IPv4 options, hosting permission, DDoS mitigation and documented repair terms. Verify independent physical routes before claiming redundancy.
- Monitor temperature, humidity, water, power and connectivity. Test alarms and controlled shutdown.
- Confirm applicable regulatory classification with Nkom and municipal requirements before commitment.
