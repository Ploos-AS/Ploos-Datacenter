# Risks and decision gates

| Risk | Mitigation / evidence required |
|---|---|
| Fiber unavailable or expensive | Written carrier offers and route assessment before site purchase |
| Grid connection cost | Written capacity and connection estimate |
| Building approval or flood exposure | Municipal and professional site assessment |
| Power or cooling failure | Tested UPS, alarms, shutdown and recovery procedures |
| Single-site outage | Off-site backup, independent DNS and recovery capacity |
| Low VPS margins | Pilot demand, measured utilization and full cost model |
| Abuse and DDoS | Acceptable use policy, response process and upstream mitigation |
| Tenant data exposure | Isolation tests, access controls and privacy review |
| Hardware failure | Spares, tested restores and capacity reserve |
| Staffing and support | Realistic service hours and escalation coverage |

Open decisions: site, ownership versus lease, module type, grid capacity, carriers, network addressing, hardware, funding and licensing. Keep sensitive evidence in restricted storage. No construction or customer SLA commitments before relevant gates are approved.
