## Minimum requirements and screening

Target 500–1000 m², or equivalent leased space, with room for a 20–30 m² first module and future expansion. A smaller serviced site may be preferable. Initial IT load target 2–5 kW; obtain professional estimates for total facility load and future expansion.

Mandatory gates before binding acquisition: permitted use and building approval path; written grid capacity and connection estimate; written commercial fiber offer allowing hosting; acceptable flood, drainage, geotechnical and access assessment; viable total project cost.

## Weighted comparison

Score each criterion 0–5 using documented evidence; unknown is unscored, not zero. Weighted score = sum(weight × score / 5). Do not rank incomplete candidates or override mandatory gates with a high score.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Fiber availability, routing and cost | 30 |
| Power availability and cost | 25 |
| Land, lease and site preparation cost | 15 |
| Planning and environmental suitability | 15 |
| Expansion potential | 10 |
| Access and maintenance logistics | 5 |

## Acquisition scenarios

At illustrative NOK 75–110/m², 500 m² costs NOK 37500–55000 and 1000 m² costs NOK 75000–110000 before fees, subdivision and site preparation. These are arithmetic scenarios, not offers for subdivided parcels.

Compare outright purchase, long-term lease, ground lease and existing industrial premises using total five-year cost, including utility connections, rent, financing, maintenance and exit costs. Do not assume subdivision is possible.

## Evidence and decision workflow

1. Request municipal confirmation of available parcels, prices, zoning, sale criteria, lease options, subdivision, development deadlines and fees.
2. Obtain indicative utility offers for each shortlisted site, including connection charges, lead times and recurring tariffs.
3. Assess flood, snow, drainage, access, noise, cooling and expansion constraints with qualified professionals.
4. Compare complete cost scenarios and record assumptions, evidence dates and unresolved questions.
5. Seek conditional reservation or option where possible; approve acquisition only after feasibility gates pass.

Keep exact facility layouts, utility routing details and confidential offers in restricted storage. Publish only non-sensitive summaries.
