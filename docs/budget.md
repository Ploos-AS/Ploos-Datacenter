Monthly model: facility kW × 730 × marginal electricity price, plus demand charges, connectivity, IP space, backup, insurance, maintenance, premises, staffing, payment fees and capital recovery. Break-even VPS count = uncovered monthly costs / net contribution per VPS. Model occupancy and CPU/RAM/storage constraints separately. Do not assume grants or heat recovery revenue.
