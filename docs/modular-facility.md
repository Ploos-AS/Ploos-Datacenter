# NO-SIR1 — Modular facility strategy

Status: feasibility only. No building, vendor or engineering design approved.

These are unverified planning ranges, not market quotations or comparable turnkey prices. Transport, foundations, utilities, installation and VAT may be additional. Compare complete installed costs with leasing existing premises.

## Initial requirements

Target approximately 20–30 m² and two rack positions, initially one populated. A nominal 6 × 4 m building is a concept only. Verify internal dimensions, door and equipment access, rack service clearances, floor loading, lifting and maintenance access. A standard shipping container may be too narrow for the required layout.

Design for initial 2–5 kW IT load and evaluate expansion to 10 kW subject to power and cooling engineering. Provide separate service areas as needed for UPS and batteries. Qualified designers must assess fire compartmentation, escape routes, ventilation, electrical protection, structural loads and safe battery installation.

Evaluate insulation, vapor control, condensation, frost, snow and wind loads, drainage, flood exposure, corrosion, noise, filtered free cooling and mechanical cooling. Avoid unnecessary water services above equipment. Plan utility entries and future module connections without publishing exact security-sensitive layouts.

## Used shelter inspection

Request age, dimensions, weight, transport and lifting instructions, structural documentation, previous use, electrical drawings, fire classification, insulation condition, water ingress history and maintenance records. Have professionals assess existing HVAC, electrical equipment, batteries and any hazardous materials before reuse. Do not assume existing installations are compliant or usable.

## Procurement

Seek budget quotations from telecom equipment decommissioning firms, modular building suppliers, container conversion companies and specialist edge datacenter vendors. Search terms: telekomhytte, fiberhytte, teknisk kiosk, sambandshytte, teknisk container, telecom shelter and BTS shelter.

Ask each supplier for delivered and installed price in Sirdal, exclusions, lead time, warranty, drawings, structural certification, cooling capacity at design conditions, electrical scope, commissioning and expansion options. Compare purchase versus lease. No suppliers have been contacted.

## Decision gates

Confirm planning permission and site conditions, written utility offers, engineering feasibility, insurance and full installed cost before purchase. Obtain municipal guidance even for movable modules. Test cooling, alarms, UPS, electrical safety and recovery before production use.

Keep detailed layouts and access arrangements restricted. Add modules only when demand, financing and utility capacity justify expansion.
