## Local funding

Sirdalsvekst KF guidelines adopted 2023-02-02, linked from the municipal website, list indicative rates:

| Category | Grant | Loan |
|---|---|---|
| Feasibility project | 25%, max NOK 250000 | Confirm |
| New private commercial building | 20%, max NOK 600000 | 25%, max NOK 1500000 |
| Building renovation | 15%, max NOK 350000 | 20%, max NOK 800000 |
| Business development with new job | 20%, max NOK 500000 | 25%, max NOK 1000000 |
| Local innovation without new job | 10%, max NOK 400000 | Confirm classification |
| Skills development | 50%, max NOK 50000 | Confirm |

Rates are discretionary, not entitlements. Confirm current rules, eligible costs, employment requirements, module classification and whether categories may be combined. The municipal English summary differs from the formal guidelines on some caps; obtain written clarification. Do not claim jobs that are not planned. Ordinary operating costs are excluded.

## Application preparation

Prepare a three-year operating and cash flow plan, itemized bids, funding plan, company accounts where available, project milestones, local value creation and credible employment projections. Disclose prior public aid and verify cumulation rules. Keep confidential accounting, bank details and supplier quotes outside public GitHub; municipal applications may be subject to public access rules.
